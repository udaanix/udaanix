Welcome to Udaanix. Udaanix (“Udaanix,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) provides multi-stream educational and career counseling services through independent counselors, including paid and unpaid guidance, assessments, mentoring, and informational resources. The Platform supports Google Account login and standard email-based registration.

This Privacy Policy is intended to reflect relevant privacy practices and applicable requirements, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India), the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), and the GDPR where applicable. By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

1. Scope and Applicability

This Policy applies to:

  • Students, parents, guardians, and learners using Udaanix services;
  • Counselors, educators, and professionals registered on the Platform; and
  • Visitors accessing Udaanix content, resources, or tools.

Please read this Policy together with our Terms & Conditions, Refund Policy, and any additional policies published on the Platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information reasonably necessary to operate the Platform, deliver counseling services, personalize the user experience, and meet legal obligations.

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Full name, age, and date of birth;
  • Email address, phone number, and communication preferences;
  • Google account profile information if you use Google Sign-In;
  • Academic background, educational qualifications, and interests;
  • Career preferences, goals, and assessment responses;
  • Booking details, session history, and communications;
  • Payment and billing information, processed through third-party payment providers; and
  • Professional credentials and verification documents for counselors.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address, device identifiers, browser type, and operating system;
  • Log data such as access times, pages viewed, session duration, and referring URLs;
  • General location information, such as city, region, or country; and
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 11).

2.3 Information from third parties

We may receive limited information from Google or other identity providers, payment processors, and service providers supporting analytics, hosting, communications, and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal data to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Platform and Services;
  • Enable account registration and authentication, including Google login;
  • Facilitate counselor–student interactions and session scheduling;
  • Personalize recommendations, assessments, and learning experiences;
  • Process payments, refunds, and subscriptions;
  • Send service-related messages, confirmations, updates, and notices;
  • Respond to inquiries, complaints, and support requests;
  • Maintain Platform safety, security, and integrity;
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud or misuse; and
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

We do not use personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects without meaningful human involvement.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, where the GDPR applies, we process personal data under one or more of these lawful bases:

  • Consent: where you have provided permission;
  • Contractual necessity: to deliver Services you request;
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable laws; and
  • Legitimate interests: to improve the Platform, protect users, and prevent fraud, where those interests do not override your rights.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

5. Authentication Using Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using your Google account, we receive basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile image. We do not access your Google password or private Google account data. Google Sign-In is used for authentication and account management. Google’s use of information is governed by Google’s own Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Udaanix does not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We may share information only:

  • With counselors you choose to interact with, to deliver services;
  • With trusted service providers for hosting, payments, analytics, communications, and security, subject to confidentiality and data-protection obligations;
  • With legal, regulatory, or government authorities when required by law;
  • To protect the rights, safety, and integrity of Udaanix, its users, or the public; or
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, subject to applicable data-protection laws.

Service providers are required to use data only for authorized purposes.

7. Confidentiality of Counseling Data

  • Counseling sessions, assessments, communications, and reports are treated as confidential.
  • Counselors are contractually required to maintain confidentiality.
  • Information may be disclosed with your consent, for safety reasons, or when legally required.

Udaanix cannot guarantee absolute confidentiality where disclosure is required by law or necessary to prevent serious harm.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and improve Services, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Data may be deleted, anonymized, or archived when no longer required. You may request deletion of eligible personal data, subject to legal and operational limitations.

9. Your Rights

9.1 Rights of Indian users

Subject to applicable law, you may be able to access and correct personal data, withdraw consent, request erasure where applicable, nominate another person to exercise rights on your behalf, and file grievances with Udaanix or the appropriate authority.

9.2 Rights of EU/EEA users under GDPR

You may request access, rectification, or deletion; restrict or object to processing; request data portability; withdraw consent; and lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority.

Requests may be submitted using the contact details in Section 16. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Children’s Privacy

  • Udaanix does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 15 years of age without parental consent.
  • Users under 18 years of age must use the Platform under parental or guardian supervision.
  • Parents or guardians may request access to, correction of, or deletion of a minor’s data, subject to applicable law.

11. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Udaanix uses cookies and similar technologies, such as pixels, SDKs, and local storage, to enable core functionality and security, remember preferences and login sessions, analyze usage trends, improve performance, and measure marketing effectiveness.

11.1 Cookie categories

  • Essential cookies: required for Platform operation and cannot be disabled through our controls.
  • Functional cookies: remember preferences and settings.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how users interact with the Platform.
  • Marketing cookies: support relevant communications and campaign measurement.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect Platform functionality.

11.2 Cookie banner wording

11.3 Cookie preference modal wording

Cookie Preferences. We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze site usage, and support marketing efforts. You can choose which categories of cookies to allow. Essential cookies are always enabled because they are required for the Platform to function properly.

  • Essential Cookies (Always Active): required for login, security, and core Platform features.

12. Marketing Communications and Consent

12.1 Students and general users

By creating an account, booking a session, submitting forms, or providing contact details, you may receive transactional and service-related communications through email, SMS, WhatsApp, or in-app notifications. These may include account confirmations, login alerts, session reminders, booking updates, receipts, invoices, and important service announcements.

With your explicit consent where required by law, we may also send promotional communications about educational programs, career resources, counseling packages, platform updates, discounts, and campaigns. You may opt out of promotional communications by using an email unsubscribe link, replying “STOP” to SMS or WhatsApp messages where supported, or changing communication preferences in your account settings. Opting out does not affect essential service-related messages.

12.2 Optional communication checkbox wording for forms

“I agree to receive service updates, session reminders, and promotional communications from Udaanix via WhatsApp, SMS, email, and phone calls, in accordance with the Privacy Policy. I understand that I can withdraw my consent at any time.”

This checkbox must be unchecked by default and requires an explicit user action.

12.3 Counselor communications

By registering as a counselor, you may receive email, SMS, WhatsApp, and in-app messages relating to account verification, onboarding, student bookings, sessions, policy and operational updates, payments, commissions, and settlements. Counselors may opt out of non-essential promotional messages but cannot opt out of critical service, legal, or operational communications needed to maintain their account.

13. International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored or processed outside your jurisdiction. Where such transfers occur, appropriate contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards are intended to be implemented, and transfers will be handled in accordance with applicable data-protection laws, including the GDPR where applicable.

14. Security Measures

Udaanix implements reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, which may include encrypted data transmission and storage, access controls and authentication protocols, secure hosting environments, and confidentiality obligations for employees, contractors, and counselors. While we strive to protect your data, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in legal requirements, business practices, technology, or Platform features. For material changes, we will notify users through the Platform or other appropriate channels. The effective date at the top indicates when this Policy was last revised. Continued use of the Platform after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
